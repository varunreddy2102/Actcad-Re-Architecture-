# Platform Brand Shortlist — "[Something]CAD"

## 0. Status

Working shortlist for the white-label platform's master brand (`docs/platform-strategy.md` §10.2). Constraint: name must end in **CAD**. Target voice: **Indian roots, modern SaaS feel, patriotic resonance in the Atmanirbhar Bharat / Modi-era register, engineering-credible to international partners (ODA, Spatial, Qt).**

**Nothing is committed yet.** This document records the candidates and the rationale. Trademark and domain clearance happen before any external use.

---

## 1. Selection criteria

| Criterion | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Indian roots** | Sanskrit / Hindi origin, readable as Indian by an Indian audience without needing translation |
| **Modern SaaS feel** | Sounds like a 2026 software platform, not a 1990s shareware product. Two syllables before "CAD" preferred; three at most |
| **Patriotic resonance** | Carries an Atmanirbhar / Modi-era / Bharat-confident association — ideally tied to a known modern symbol (HAL Tejas LCA, ISRO missions, Bharat Nirmaan, Setu Bharatam) |
| **Engineering credibility** | A Spatial / ODA / Qt commercial counterparty must read it as a serious engineering brand, not a consumer app |
| **International pronounceability** | Members may be in India, SEA, EU, NA, LATAM. The name must survive being spoken in English by people who don't know Sanskrit |
| **TM / domain clearance potential** | Distinct enough from existing CAD vendors and Indian SaaS brands to clear trademark in target jurisdictions and acquire a usable `.com` / `.cad` / `.io` |
| **Sub-brand room** | Master brand should support a family: `[X] Engine`, `[X] Forge` (marketplace), `[X] Cloud` (SaaS surface), `[X] SDK` |

---

## 2. Tier 1 — strongest fit

The four names that land **all seven criteria** at full strength.

### 2.1 **TejasCAD**

**Sanskrit:** तेजस् — *brilliance, fire, radiant sharpness*

**Patriotic anchor:** HAL Tejas LCA — India's first indigenously designed fighter jet, **the living symbol of Atmanirbhar Bharat** in engineering. The name is recognized in defense, aerospace, and political contexts as shorthand for "Indian-engineered alternative to imported tech."

**Why it works for this platform:**

- The Tejas LCA story is exactly the story we're telling: *India-engineered alternative to Autodesk, Bricsys, etc., built on Indian terms with Indian IP ownership.*
- Two syllables before "CAD" → clean rhythm: **Te-jas-CAD**.
- Sharp, fast, masculine-coded but not heavy. Reads as fast / precise — the right associations for a CAD platform.
- International CXOs at Spatial / ODA / Qt will read the reference correctly; it does the patriotic positioning for us in a single word.

**Sub-brand family:**

- **TejasCAD** — master brand, the platform
- **Tejas Engine** — the C++ runtime / core engine
- **Tejas Forge** — the plugin marketplace
- **Tejas Cloud** — the SaaS / co-edit / AI surface
- **Tejas SDK** — developer-facing extension toolkit

**Watch-outs:**

- **TM exposure:** "Tejas" is heavily used in India (HAL itself uses it; many Indian SaaS startups; a personal name). TM clearance for **TejasCAD** specifically should clear because no CAD vendor uses the compound, but the bare **Tejas** mark is busy. Recommendation: register **TejasCAD** + the sub-brand family compounds; do not attempt to own bare "Tejas."
- **Domain:** `tejascad.com` to be checked. Likely available. `.cad` TLD doesn't exist; `tejas.io` is taken.
- **Pronunciation:** stress on first syllable in Sanskrit (TEH-jus); some Westerners will say "TAY-jus." Both work.

**Recommendation rank: #1.**

### 2.2 **NirmaanCAD**

**Sanskrit / Hindi:** निर्माण — *construction, creation, building, manufacture*

**Patriotic anchor:** "**Bharat Nirmaan**" — the multi-decade nation-building idiom, formalized as a flagship rural infrastructure program. "Nation-building" in Indian political discourse is *Rashtra Nirmaan*. The word carries a "we are building the future" resonance natively.

**Why it works for this platform:**

- **Literal vertical fit.** CAD is the tool of *nirmaan*. The brand reads as "the tool that builds the buildings." No semantic gap for AEC customers.
- Three syllables before "CAD" → **Nir-maan-CAD**. Slightly longer than Tejas but the rhythm is fine.
- Tech-context-friendly: Razorpay uses a "Nirmaan" sub-brand; the name is already proven to work in modern SaaS.

**Sub-brand family:**

- **NirmaanCAD** — master brand
- **Nirmaan Engine** — runtime
- **Nirmaan Forge** — marketplace
- **Nirmaan Cloud** — SaaS surface

**Watch-outs:**

- Common Hindi word → lower TM distinctiveness on the bare word. **NirmaanCAD** compound is much more defensible.
- Pronunciation: foreign speakers may say "Nir-MAHN" vs "NIR-mahn." Acceptable variance.
- Spelling: "Nirmaan" vs "Nirman" — pick one in writing and never deviate. Recommendation: **Nirmaan** (double-a preserves the long vowel and reads more correctly for non-Hindi speakers).

**Recommendation rank: #2.**

### 2.3 **GaganCAD**

**Sanskrit / Hindi:** गगन — *sky, heavens, firmament*

**Patriotic anchor:** **Gaganyaan** — India's first crewed spaceflight mission, **the Modi-era ISRO flagship.** Direct association with India's most ambitious current engineering project.

**Why it works for this platform:**

- "Sky / horizon" framing positions the brand as *the future of CAD*, not just another DWG editor.
- Two syllables before "CAD" → **Ga-gan-CAD**. Easy to say, easy to remember.
- Modern but rooted; not religious, not loaded politically beyond the ISRO association.
- The Gaganyaan connection signals "Indian engineering at the frontier" without being on-the-nose.

**Sub-brand family:**

- **GaganCAD** — master brand
- **Gagan Engine** — runtime
- **Gagan Forge** — marketplace
- **Gagan Cloud** — SaaS surface (the "cloud" pun is intentional and good)

**Watch-outs:**

- "Gagan" is a personal name in India — moderate TM background traffic.
- The Gaganyaan reference may date if the mission slips — but the underlying meaning ("sky") is timeless, so brand survives the news cycle.
- `gagancad.com` to be checked.

**Recommendation rank: #3.**

### 2.4 **VikramCAD**

**Sanskrit:** विक्रम — *valor, prowess, stride; also the name of the legendary king Vikramaditya*

**Patriotic anchor:** **Vikram lander** (Chandrayaan-2, Chandrayaan-3) — the lander that put India on the Moon's south pole in 2023. Modi-era space patriotism at peak intensity.

**Why it works for this platform:**

- The Chandrayaan-3 success is the single most emotionally resonant Indian engineering moment of the 2020s. The brand inherits that mood.
- Two syllables before "CAD" → **Vik-ram-CAD**. Strong consonant rhythm.
- "Vikramaditya" (the king) also carries a Bharat-civilizational confidence.

**Watch-outs:**

- "Vikram" is one of the most common Indian male personal names → TM distinctiveness on the compound is fine, but the bare word is hopelessly contested.
- Could be misread as a personal product ("Vikram's CAD") more than the others.

**Recommendation rank: #4.**

## 3. Tier 2 — strong engineering signal, softer patriotism

Names that lead with engineering credibility; the patriotism is via reclaiming classical Indian engineering vocabulary rather than tying to a current symbol.

| Name | Root | Why it could work | Why it's not Tier 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **YantraCAD** | यन्त्र — *instrument, machine, engineering device* | Pure engineering signal; Yantra is the Sanskrit word for any precision device. Distinctly Indian. | Parallel meaning is "mystic diagram" — some Western readers may mis-read as new-age. Manageable with positioning. |
| **AakarCAD** | आकार — *form, shape* | Direct CAD-vocabulary fit (CAD literally creates *aakaar*). Two syllables, modern feel. | Weakest patriotic punch in the list. Reads as "smart" more than "patriotic." |
| **ShilpaCAD** | शिल्प — *craft, sculpture, architecture* (Shilpa Shastra = classical Indian architectural science) | Architecture / AEC fit; ancient Indian engineering reference. | "Shilpa" is a very common Indian feminine personal name → low TM distinctiveness, mild personal-product mis-read. |
| **SrishtiCAD** | सृष्टि — *creation, the act of creating* | Creation / making fit; feminine-coded but tech-context-neutral. | Common personal name; patriotic resonance is generic-Hindu rather than Modi-era. |
| **VimaanCAD** | विमान — *flying chariot, aircraft* | Aerospace / aviation; classical Indian aerospace reference (vimana in ancient texts). | Aerospace-vertical narrow; not ideal for AEC framing. |

## 4. Tier 3 — defense-themed, very strong patriotism, heavier voice

Names tied to Indian defense / missile / weapon systems. **Highest patriotic intensity** but heavier in tone — these say "Atmanirbhar Bharat military" rather than "modern SaaS."

| Name | Root + association | Recommend? |
|---|---|---|
| **AstraCAD** | अस्त्र — weapon; Astra is the BVRAAM missile system | Strong patriotism; aggressive voice; may feel too defense-coded for civilian AEC customers |
| **AkashCAD** | आकाश — sky; also the Akash surface-to-air missile | Lighter than Astra; sky-association makes it dual-readable (positive) |
| **VajraCAD** | वज्र — thunderbolt, Indra's weapon, also "diamond" | Sharp, strong; "diamond" reading is interesting (hardness, precision) |
| **AgniCAD** | अग्नि — fire; Agni ICBM missile family | Heavy, intense; probably too aggressive for the brand |
| **ShaktiCAD** | शक्ति — power, energy, the divine feminine | Modi-era resonance (women empowerment + Shakti nuclear test); positive valence |

Tier 3 is recorded for completeness but not recommended unless the brand voice explicitly wants the defense / strength register. **For a partner-recruitment platform brand, Tier 1 is the right register.**

## 5. Names considered and set aside

| Name | Why set aside |
|---|---|
| **BharatCAD** | Too literal. Reads as a government scheme, not a private platform |
| **IndiaCAD** | Same, worse — feels like a regional sub-product of someone else's CAD |
| **DesiCAD** | Informal register, undermines engineering credibility |
| **SetuCAD** | Setu metaphor is great, but `setu.co` (Indian fintech) is established → TM conflict |
| **PragatiCAD** | "Progress" is too generic; used by many gov programs |
| **AdityaCAD** | Aditya-L1 reference is current but the Hindu sun-god association may read religious in some markets |
| **AryaCAD** | "Arya" is politically and historically loaded internationally → reputational risk |
| **PranavCAD** | Pranav = the primordial Om; explicitly religious → risk for international B2B |
| **VedaCAD / VedicCAD** | Religious-coded; risk |
| **RashtraCAD** | "Rashtra" = nation; correct meaning but reads as government / political party brand |

---

## 6. Recommendation in one sentence

**TejasCAD** is the strongest fit on every criterion; **NirmaanCAD** is the strongest fit specifically for the AEC vertical positioning; **GaganCAD** is the strongest forward-looking (sky / future / Gaganyaan) framing. **My recommendation is TejasCAD with the `Tejas Engine` / `Tejas Forge` / `Tejas Cloud` / `Tejas SDK` sub-brand family** — it carries the Atmanirbhar Bharat story in a single word, reads internationally, supports a clean sub-brand structure, and clears TM as a compound even though the bare "Tejas" mark is contested.

## 7. Next steps when a direction is picked

1. **TM clearance** in IN, US, EU, SG, AU, AE for the chosen mark + sub-brand family
2. **Domain acquisition** — `.com` is primary; `.io`, `.tech`, `.cad-tld-if-exists`, `.in` as defenses
3. **Common-law search** — Google, GitHub, Producthunt, Crunchbase for existing usage
4. **Linguistic check** — surface negative meanings in target languages (Tamil, Marathi, Bengali, plus EU and SEA languages)
5. **Sub-brand lock** — decide the family naming pattern (`[Name] Engine` vs `[Name]Core`, `[Name] Forge` vs `[Name] Market`) and apply consistently across all artifacts
6. Then — and only then — update `docs/platform-strategy.md` §10.2, `docs/exec-presentation.md`, and the README with the chosen brand
